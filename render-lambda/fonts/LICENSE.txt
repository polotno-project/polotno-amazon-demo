The .ttf files in this folder are the Liberation Fonts, version 2.1.5.

They are metric-compatible replacements for Arial, Times New Roman and
Courier New. AWS Lambda has no system fonts, so these are shipped inside the
deployment package. See fonts.conf for how fontconfig finds them.
